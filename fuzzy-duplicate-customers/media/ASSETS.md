# Visual assets

Sheet images are renders of the included review workbooks. The hero combines the Before and After renders with the shared casebook layout. No external photographs or illustrations are used.

Embedded lettering uses Inter, Instrument Serif and JetBrains Mono under the SIL Open Font License. Their licence notices are retained in [Inter](font-licenses/LICENSE-Inter.txt), [Instrument Serif](font-licenses/LICENSE-InstrumentSerif.txt) and [JetBrains Mono](font-licenses/LICENSE-JetBrainsMono.txt). Fonts are used in raster images; font binaries are not distributed here.
